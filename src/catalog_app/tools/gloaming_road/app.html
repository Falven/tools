Match your guard to the space the enemy’s blade enters: your left is not their left. Begin the correct guard just before contact to parry. A held guard blocks, but costs stamina. A wrong-side or late guard does not protect you.

Heavy blows press a guard. Defend during the early windup to feint; active strikes and recovery cannot be cancelled. Dodge out of the blade’s actual path—there is no invulnerability. Rest at a quiet stone hearth to recover.

A deliberate mouse gesture also changes your persistent guard; small aim movements do not. Keyboard-only play: arrow keys look, J attacks, K defends. Menus support Tab, arrows, Enter and controller navigation. In an App host that blocks mouse capture, use these keyboard controls or a controller.
