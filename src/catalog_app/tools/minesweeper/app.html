    Minesweeper Help

    A classic for a reason.

    Reveal every safe tile. Leave the mines alone. Your first reveal and its neighbors are always safe.

    1. 01
      Read between the mines.A number tells you how many mines touch that tile, including diagonals. Empty areas open automatically.
    2. 02
      Plant a flag. Not a foot.Right-click, long-press, or switch to Flag mode. On a keyboard, use arrows to move, Enter / Space to reveal, and F to flag.
    3. 03
      Clear a little faster.Once a number has that many flags beside it, click the number to reveal its remaining neighbors. Incorrect flags can cost you the game!
    10 points per safe tile + 1,000 for a win + up to 1,000 speed points.
    Beginner ×1 · Intermediate ×2 · Expert ×3.
    Speed bonus = max(0, 1,000 − elapsed seconds).
    Only wins enter the Hall of Fame. Your best score counts; ties go to the faster clear.

    The timer starts on your first reveal and keeps running while you read help or leave the tab. Scores are shared only for this server session and disappear on restart.
